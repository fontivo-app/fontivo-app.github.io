# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **Fontivo** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for designers, developers, and users of Fontivo

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, publishing private information without permission, or other unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `ahmedfahmy8308@gmail.com`. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
